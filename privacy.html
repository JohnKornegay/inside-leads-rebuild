PRIVACY POLICY

What we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy explains what information Inside Leads ("we", "us") collects when you visit getinsideleads.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

When you submit our contact form, we collect the details you enter, such as your name, email address, phone number, company, website, the service you are interested in, your budget range and your message. When you email or call us, we receive the information you choose to share.

Information collected automatically

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site, such as which pages are viewed, how long visits last, the general location derived from your IP address, and your device and browser type. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies to do this. Our free tools run in your browser; some, such as the Google review QR code generator, send the text you enter to a third-party service to produce the result.

How we use information

  • To respond to your inquiry and provide the services you request
  • To improve the website and understand which content is useful
  • To keep the site secure and prevent abuse

We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

We use trusted providers to run the site: Vercel (hosting), Supabase (storing contact form submissions) and Google Analytics (website analytics). They process information on our behalf and only as needed to provide their services.

Your choices

  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.
  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete the information you submitted through our contact form by emailing john@getinsideleads.com.

Data retention

We keep contact form submissions for as long as needed to respond to you and manage any working relationship, and delete them on request.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy from time to time and will change the date above when we do. Questions? Email john@getinsideleads.com or call (313) 782-5835.
