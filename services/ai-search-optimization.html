AI Search Optimization

AI SEARCH OPTIMIZATION

Customers increasingly ask AI tools who to hire. We help your business show up and get cited in Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity and Copilot answers.

Who this is for

Local and service businesses that rank reasonably well in Google but rarely get mentioned when someone asks an AI assistant for a recommendation, and businesses that want to be ready as more searches end in an AI-generated answer instead of a click.

What's included

  • AI visibility baseline: we test the questions your customers actually ask across Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity, and record who gets mentioned and which sources get cited.
  • Answer-first content: service pages and guides restructured so each section opens with a clear, quotable answer, with specific facts, prices, timelines and locations where they apply.
  • Entity and structured data: Organization, Person, Service and LocalBusiness schema that ties your business, people, services and locations together, plus consistent name, address and phone details everywhere.
  • Third-party corroboration: the directories, review platforms, industry lists and community sources AI tools draw on to decide who to recommend, with your profiles complete and consistent.
  • Technical access for AI crawlers: a deliberate robots.txt policy, server-rendered content that crawlers can read, a clean sitemap and an llms.txt summary of your business.
  • Measurement: AI referral traffic in GA4, citation tracking for your question set, and the classic SEO metrics that still drive most AI answers.

How we work

We start with an audit of how AI tools describe your business today and which competitors and sources they cite instead. Then we fix the foundations first: crawl access, structured data and consistent business information. After that we rework your most important pages for answer-first clarity and build the off-site corroboration that makes AI tools confident recommending you.

Because AI answers change often, reporting is monthly and based on a fixed set of real customer questions, so you can see the trend rather than a single snapshot.

Frequently asked questions

What is AI search optimization?

It is the work of making your business easy for AI-powered search tools to find, understand and cite. That includes Google AI Overviews and AI Mode, ChatGPT, Perplexity and Microsoft Copilot. It builds on classic SEO rather than replacing it, because most AI answers draw on pages that already rank and on sources the web already trusts.

Is this different from regular SEO?

It overlaps heavily. Crawlable pages, strong content and links still matter. The difference is emphasis: AI tools quote short, self-contained passages, rely on structured data and consistent business information, and lean on third-party sources such as directories, reviews and community discussions to decide who to recommend.

Can you guarantee my business will be cited by ChatGPT?

No one can. AI answers vary by question, location and model, and they change often. What we can do is remove the reasons you are being skipped, strengthen the signals these tools rely on, and measure how often you appear for the questions your customers actually ask.

How do you measure AI search visibility?

We track a set of real customer questions across Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Perplexity, and record whether you are mentioned or cited, alongside the classic signals: rankings, Search Console impressions, referral traffic from AI tools in GA4, and branded search growth.

Should I block AI crawlers from my site?

For most local businesses, no. If AI tools cannot read your site, they cannot cite it. We review your robots.txt with you and make a deliberate choice about search, AI answers and AI training.
