Local SEO Services

LOCAL SEO

Get found in the Google map pack and local search results by the customers closest to you. Local SEO for service businesses in Raleigh, NC, Detroit, MI and beyond.

Who local SEO is for

Local SEO is for businesses whose customers search with a place in mind, like "near me", a city name, or a neighborhood. Home services, med spas and clinics, real estate teams, restaurants, and professional services all win or lose a large share of their leads in the map pack and the first few organic results.

What's included

  • Google Business Profile optimization: primary and secondary categories, services, business description, hours, photos and regular posts, so the profile is complete, accurate and attractive.
  • Review generation and management: a simple system for asking every happy customer for a review, plus prompt, professional responses to every review, positive or negative.
  • Citations and NAP consistency: your name, address and phone number matched across the directories Google and AI assistants rely on, such as Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Yelp, BBB and industry directories.
  • Local landing pages: service pages and location pages built around what local customers actually search for, without thin city-swap pages.
  • Local schema markup: structured data that tells search engines and AI systems who you are, what you do and where you work.
  • Tracking and reporting: calls, direction requests, form fills and rankings measured in Google Analytics, Search Console and your Business Profile.

How we work

Every engagement starts with an audit of your Business Profile, reviews, citations, website and the local competitors you are up against. From there we fix the foundations first, then build the review program and local content that compound over time.

Monthly reporting shows what changed, what it produced, and what we are doing next. For a walkthrough of a full six-month plan, see the HVAC local SEO example engagement.

Frequently asked questions

How long does local SEO take to show results?

Google Business Profile fixes such as categories, services and photos can change map visibility within weeks. Review volume, citations and location content build over months. We set expectations per market after the audit rather than promising a timeline up front.

Do I need a physical address to rank in the map pack?

No. Service-area businesses can verify a Google Business Profile with the address hidden and list the areas they serve. The profile still needs to be verified, and proximity to the searcher still matters.

Do you serve businesses outside Raleigh and Detroit?

Yes. Most of our local SEO clients are in the Raleigh-Durham-Chapel Hill area and metro Detroit, but the work is done remotely and applies to any local market.

Will you create pages for every nearby town?

Only where there is something real to say about that town, such as jobs completed, reviews or local specifics. Thin city-swap pages can be treated as doorway pages by Google, so we avoid them.
